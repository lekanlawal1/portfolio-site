Superstore Margin Console: Retail BI Dashboard

A profitability audit for a retailer whose revenue grew every year while nearly 1 in 5 order lines lost money.

PythonpandasPlotly.js vanilla JSGitHub Pages

The story

A US retailer's sales climbed steadily from 2015–2018, the chart every executive loves. But overall margin sat at 12.5%, and 18% of order lines actually lost money. Leadership could see the growth; nobody could see where profit was leaking. This dashboard is the audit that answers it: which products, discount levels, and regions are quietly funding growth with losses.

The pipeline is a scripted, reproducible clean: every transformation writes to a decision log explaining what was done and why, from dropping 806 phantom rows created by a multi-sheet export to keeping a $22.6K outlier order because real revenue isn't noise. Row-level data is exported into the page itself, so every KPI, chart, and the product table recompute live in the browser as you search and filter, with no backend, free hosting, instant load.

Key decisions I'd defend in an interview

  • Static Plotly.js over Power BI: a public, link-accessible demo with zero licensing cost; Power BI public embedding needs a paid workspace.
  • Margin, not raw profit, as the comparison unit: raw profit rewards big categories; margin exposes the discount cliff.
  • Flag, don't drop, suspect rows: rows failing logistics checks keep their valid revenue but are excluded from shipping metrics only.
  • Recommendation shipped with the numbers: cap discounts at 20% with manager approval above it; renegotiate or de-emphasize Tables.
