Fine Print · interactive demo

The AI reads. The code decides.

Fine Print is an iPhone app that checks leases, job offers and terms of service against Ontario law. Pick a sample below to see what it finds, with the law cited for every verdict, or open Check your own document and paste one in. Then try Try to break it and switch off the safety check that stops a wrong legal verdict.

2Code checks the AIThe quote must exist in the document, and its wording must support the label the AI gave it.

What is real here, and what isn't

  • Real: the documents, the rule packs (with citations and verified dates) and the verdict logic. Verdicts are computed in your browser by the app's rule engine and label check, ported from Swift to JavaScript and tested against the app's own rule tests (97 checks, all passing).
  • Samples are pre-recorded: for the sample documents, step 1 uses the clause tags from the app's evaluation run, where Gemini found every one of them (34 of 34 clauses, 20 of 20 numbers read correctly). Nothing is sent anywhere when you browse them.
  • Your own document is live: Gemini reads it through a small server that holds the API key and stores nothing. Quote checks, label checks and verdicts then run in your browser, exactly as for the samples.
  • Not legal advice. Fine Print points to the law and says what it covers; it does not replace a lawyer, a tenant clinic or the Landlord and Tenant Board.
