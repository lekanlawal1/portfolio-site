Fine Print · interactive demo

The AI reads. The code decides.

Fine Print is an iPhone app that checks leases, job offers and terms of service against Ontario law. Pick a document below to see what it finds, with the law cited for every verdict. Then open Try to break it and switch off the safety check that stops a wrong legal verdict.

1AI reads and quotesThe model tags clauses from a closed list and must quote each one word for word. It never gives a verdict.
2Code checks the AIThe quote must exist in the document, and its wording must support the label the AI gave it.
3Cited rules judgeOnly then do rules from the Residential Tenancies Act and Employment Standards Act apply, each with its section.

What is real here, and what isn't

  • Real: the documents, the rule packs (with citations and verified dates) and the verdict logic. Verdicts are computed in your browser by the app's rule engine and label check, ported from Swift to JavaScript and tested against the app's own rule tests (97 checks, all passing).
  • Pre-recorded: step 1. In the app, Gemini or Apple's on-device model reads the document live. Here the clause tags are the ones from the app's evaluation run, where Gemini found every one of them (34 of 34 clauses, 20 of 20 numbers read correctly). This keeps the demo free and private: nothing you do here is sent anywhere.
  • Not legal advice. Fine Print points to the law and says what it covers; it does not replace a lawyer, a tenant clinic or the Landlord and Tenant Board.
