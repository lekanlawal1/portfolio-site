Shipment and Inventory Discrepancy Checker

An Excel workbook for warehouse and 3PL teams. On the sample data it finds 9 count variances worth $502.70, 8 receiving exceptions and $1,028 of billing errors, then puts them on one dashboard. Change a blue number below and watch every check update.

Download the workbook ↓ Try the checks

Sample data. Every company, SKU, quantity, rate and dollar amount here is made up. Carrier names are real Canadian carriers, used only to make the receiving log realistic.

Counts vs the system

Variances are priced, not just counted, so a missing $174 case of hoodies outranks a missing $4 sleeve of cup lids.

Receipts vs slip and PO

A receipt can match its packing slip and still be wrong: the supplier may have shipped, and billed, a different quantity than was ordered.

Invoices vs the rate card

Overbilling and underbilling are reported separately, so they can't cancel out, with a $1 tolerance so rounding cents don't create noise.

What layout does it need?

Same sheet names and column headers as the sample workbook (row 1). Any sheet you leave out is skipped.

  • Inventory: SKU, System qty, Physical count. Optional: Reason, Count date.
  • Receiving: PO, SKU, PO qty, Packing slip qty, Qty received. Optional: Receipt date, Carrier, BOL.
  • Billing: Month, Client, Pallets stored, Invoice amount. Rate comes from Lists, or a Rate column.
  • Items (optional): SKU, Description, Bin location, Unit cost.
  • Lists (optional): Client and Storage rate columns, and a Billing tolerance cell ($1.00 if missing).
