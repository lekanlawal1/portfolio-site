Using your own file

Sheets are matched by name and columns by their header in row 1. Any sheet you leave out is skipped.
  • Inventory: SKU, System qty, Physical count. Optional: Reason, Count date.
  • Receiving: PO, SKU, PO qty, Packing slip qty, Qty received. Optional: Receipt date, Carrier, BOL.
  • Billing: Month, Client, Pallets stored, Invoice amount. Rate comes from the Lists sheet, or a Rate column.
  • Items (optional): SKU, Description, Bin location, Unit cost. Needed for descriptions and dollar variance.
  • Lists (optional): Client and Storage rate columns, and a Billing tolerance cell. Tolerance defaults to $1.00.
