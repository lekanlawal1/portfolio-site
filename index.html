About

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Trent University (2026) looking for data, BI and AI analyst roles in Canada. What I care about is the judgment layer: not just making a chart or calling an API, but knowing why each cleaning decision was made, what a model's honest ceiling is, and what a business should actually do differently on Monday.

One idea runs through all of them: the AI proposes, code checks. The ticket triage tool catches its model when it is wrong. The football agent's SQL is checked twice and run in the open, so every number can be verified. Fine Print takes it to an iPhone app: the AI reads and quotes, and code decides. The Canada Economy platform puts it into production, and the Discrepancy Checker brings it to the warehouse floor in the tool operations teams already use: Excel.
